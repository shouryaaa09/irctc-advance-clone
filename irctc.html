Understanding Train Classes

1AC
First Class AC
  • 4 berths per cabin with door
  • AC with individual controls
  • Bedding & meals included
  • Most expensive option
2AC
Second Class AC
  • 6 berths per bay with curtain
  • AC with comfortable berths
  • Bedding included
  • Good balance of comfort & price
3AC
Third Class AC
  • 8 berths per bay (open)
  • AC with basic comfort
  • Bedding included
  • Most popular AC option
SL
Sleeper Class
  • 8 berths per bay (open)
  • Non-AC with fans
  • No bedding provided
  • Most economical for long trips
CC
AC Chair Car
  • Reclining seats (no berths)
  • AC with charging points
  • Best for day journeys
  • Comfortable seating
2S
Second Sitting
  • Regular seats (no berths)
  • Non-AC with fans
  • Best for short journeys
  • Most economical option
